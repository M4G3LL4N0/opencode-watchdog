# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities by emailing security@noaerth.com.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and will provide a
detailed response within 5 business days outlining the next steps in handling
the report.

After your initial report, we will keep you informed of the progress towards
fixing the issue and provide an estimated timeline for a complete resolution.

We ask that you keep the report confidential and not disclose the vulnerability
to any third party until we have had an opportunity to address it.
